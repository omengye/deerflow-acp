# Automatic candidate discovery

Discovery runs under the native automatic policy and consumes only fresh,
authorized host evidence. Retired work is visible as history and never replays.
A root's retries, subagents and resume operations share one independence key;
separate roots in one chat remain independent.

A normalized pattern ledger retains all members, conflicts and withdrawals.
Representative context samples contain at most 32 members, while scans advance
in pages of 128. Neither size limits lifetime capacity. Matching pins the
authorized catalog, stores metadata exclusions and body comparisons, and reads
bodies in groups of eight. A new capability requires complete coverage with no
unresolved possible match. Discovery rechecks live evidence before generation,
validation and publication; an old source digest grants no new authority.

Candidates are complete immutable packages. The host selects writable sources,
validates portable names, preserves owner tombstones and rejects credentials or
permission escalation. There is no special new-skill setting or approval step.
New skills and existing updates use the same trusted behavior pipeline, including
held-out examples, positive/negative routing and actual OCI execution for scripts.
Insufficient evidence or runtime capability waits for a meaningful change.

One target has one active adoption at a time. A failed new-skill attempt can have
a linked successor only after at least two fresh independent ledger members;
consumed episodes cannot vote again. Explicit rejection, adoption or deletion is
not refunded. Cooldown, fairness, bounded workers and three total transient
attempts control work; daily calls, token budgets and publication quotas do not.
Paid output and phase progress persist before staging or committing a decision.

Automatic candidates cannot be approved by a manual tool, copied through a
publication edit or reimported to bypass validation. Durable content provenance
survives deletion of proposal metadata. Publication uses CAS and workspace routing;
post-publication observations use real host outcomes, not evaluation successes.

See [behavior.md](behavior.md) for validation and
[the final plan](../../../../docs/skill-evolution-final-plan-20261109.md) for
migration, observation and retention contracts. Historical record decoding is
read-only and is independent of the removed configuration contract.
